## Relationship to SynapseLab

These papers provide scientific motivation and context.

They do not validate the exact numerical results produced by SynapseLab.

The 40% interference result shown by the application comes from the project's simplified computational rule:

New Weight = Old Weight × 0.6

Therefore:

Simulated interference = 40%

This is a property of the simulation and should not be interpreted as a biological measurement.
